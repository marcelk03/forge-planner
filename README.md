# Forge planner

Open `index.html` in your browser. No installation or internet needed. Keep the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files together.

Forge Level means the completed level: **20 + Started = upgrading 20 to 21**. Not started includes the full cost and duration of level 21 and every later upgrade. Started assumes level 21 has already been paid for, and replaces only its duration with your entered remaining time. All later times and costs remain in the projection.

Selecting Started starts the countdown automatically. Enter the remaining time shown in your game, such as `2d 3h 15m`; do not apply the multiplier again. Results update every second. Pause/resume is available. Changing Forge Level clears the previous upgrade state. At zero, future upgrades remain included; update your completed level after collecting the upgrade.

Inputs and upgrade state persist in browser storage. Old saved forge levels are converted once from the old target-level convention to completed level (21 becomes 20). The old total-time countdown is discarded. Defaults use completed level 20. Restore defaults also clears upgrade state.

The original spreadsheet remains unchanged. Max Level only affects gold per hammer; the lookup table continues through 140. Hammer Thief Hammers remains unused, as in the sheet. Ascension cost remains 3,000,000 per remaining ascension up to 3. Projected income is future income only; the app does not track existing gold or hammers.
