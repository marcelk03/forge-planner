How it works & level table

Cash: The cash target is the full gold price of the next unpaid forge upgrade. At level 20, Not started reserves cash for 20 -> 21. When that upgrade is Started and paid, the cash target moves to 21 -> 22.

Hammers: The hammer target covers costs and income only after the cash-reserved upgrade finishes. For cash 21 -> 22, it covers 22 -> 23 onward and remaining ascensions. Earnings during the current and cash-reserved upgrades help you collect the targets; they are not deducted in advance. Hammers are rounded up, with a minimum of zero.

Time: Total time to max includes the current countdown and all later upgrades. The cash and hammer targets stay fixed as that countdown runs. Changing level, upgrade status, costs, or income bonuses recalculates them. No resources are automatically credited. The projection assumes continuous upgrades and average income; intermediate cash availability is not guaranteed.

Rates: Daily gold = Hammer Thief Gold + Offline Gold × 86,400 + League Gold / 7 + Clan Gold. Daily hammers = Hammer Thief Hammers + Offline Hammer × 1,440 + (League Hammer + Clan Hammer) / 7. Gold per hammer = 20 × Sell Price × 1.01Max Level / Free Forge.

Target: Costs and time include upgrades through forge level 140. Forge Cost and Forge Timer multiply base costs and durations. Max Level affects gold per hammer. Each remaining ascension, up to three, adds 3,000,000 gold. Update Forge Level after an upgrade completes.
